Given the same normalized snapshot and clock, every registered detector produces the same output. The core recommendation path is offline and local.

Four mixed rules

Stable replay of variable upstream evidence

Shadow-call and model-eval verdicts may vary when generated. Once stored, the four consuming detectors replay them byte-stably.

Optional prose

User-initiated or explicitly gated

Local-model analysis and the server audit judge can generate variable prose. They are separate surfaces and cannot silently upgrade a rule into proof.

Proof runs

Variable agents; reproducible objective machinery

Agent outputs—and any model tie-breaker—can vary. Objective gates, statistics, and stop rules are deterministic; the receipt records the bounded result and uncertainty.

Claim boundary

A repeatable detector can still encode an author-specific regex, threshold, task taxonomy, or prescription. Auditability establishes reproducibility; only a broader cohort can test external validity.

Appendix K

A receipt is a bounded claim.

1

Premise

Did the recommendation fire on meaningful real history, with enough economic or quality headroom to test?

2

Design

What was preregistered? Which fixtures, arms, model version, repetitions, MDE, and stop rules?

3

Gates

Did both arms build, test, preserve required files, and satisfy the objective outcome contract?

4

Effect

What changed, with what uncertainty? Proven, null, refuted, stale, or revoked?

Distributional, not magical: a receipt applies to a model version, task population, intervention, and time. It is not a guarantee for every future run.
Model churn creates work: a changed model can invalidate model-sensitive confidence. The future design should rerun, restamp, or revoke the result.
Current proof result: an honest null, separately recomputed in an adversarial project review. This was not independent third-party validation and does not establish the economic thesis.

Appendix P

Likely objections

“Anthropic will ship this.”

They will absorb more telemetry and coaching. The bet is the independent proof/revalidation role across vendors, not ownership of charts.

“Another startup can copy it.”

Yes. The current moat is not features. History and receipts could compound, but trust and generalizability still have to be earned.

“Isn’t this overfit to you?”

Possibly. The engine is reproducible, not proven unbiased. Freeze the rules, compare author and cohort firing, publish every misfire, then tune and re-test on unseen histories.

“Where are the savings?”

Not proven yet. The tool has measured spend and found testable levers. The first formal receipt was null. The pilot decides whether a useful proof is worth funding.

“Why not just use OTel?”

OTel transports events. It does not decide which behavior to change, prove causality, or keep model-scoped recommendation receipts.

“Why no proxy?”

A proxy raises latency, credential, and critical-path risk before evidence justifies it. Retrospective artifacts and hooks cover the current coaching job.
